# Punishments This page outlines various punishments applicable under different circumstances. ## Types of Punishments 1. **Warning**: A verbal or written warning that is issued for minor offenses. 2. **Suspension**: Temporary removal from participation or access to resources. 3. **Termination**: Permanent removal from participation or access due to severe violations. ## Purpose of Punishments The purpose of these punishments is to ensure fair play and adherence to the established rules. They serve as a deterrent to maintain a respectful environment.
