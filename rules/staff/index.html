Staff Rules

Welcome to the team! These are the official staff rules that govern our conduct within the organization.

1. Maintain Professionalism

All staff members are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner at all times.

2. Respect Confidentiality

Confidential information should be protected and not disclosed without proper authorization.

3. Communicate Effectively

Communication should be respectful, clear, and constructive.

4. Follow Policies

All staff members must adhere to company policies and procedures.

5. Report Issues

If you encounter any issues, report them to your supervisor immediately.
